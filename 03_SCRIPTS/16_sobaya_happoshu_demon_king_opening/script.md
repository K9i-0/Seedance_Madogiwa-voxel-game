# Game Opening Video: "Sobaya, the Happoshu Demon King"

## Format

- Length: 30 seconds
- Structure: 5 clips x 6 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Genre: comedic fantasy game opening / office slice-of-life
- Visual style: polished high-end anime game cinematic with bold readable silhouettes, warm izakaya colors, and harmless cartoon effects
- Output design: every clip uses a fixed first frame and last frame for CapCut / Seedance 2.0 first-last-frame generation.

## Story summary

At a cozy fictional izakaya, Sobaya orders a beer. A server secretly substitutes happoshu, assuming nobody will notice. Sobaya takes one sip, recognizes the substitution instantly, and his mandatory mask turns blazing red. A harmless cartoon explosion transforms the izakaya into a theatrical fantasy throne room. Wearing a dark demon-king cape over his unchanged short-sleeve T-shirt, Sobaya raises his mandatory giant beer mug like a royal scepter and declares himself the new enemy of the Window-Side Tribe. Tokun, Yotan, Fukuchan, and Yametaro answer the challenge with delighted battle-ready smiles. This is a real event and the opening of the game, not a dream.

## Character and design invariants

- Sobaya: 41-year-old, 180 cm, 100 kg, tall muscular adult man. His full mask, short-sleeve T-shirt, and giant glass beer mug are mandatory in every shot. The mask begins white, turns vivid red from cartoon anger, and remains a physical full-face mask. A removable demon-king cape and small crown may be added over his unchanged design.
- Tokun: slightly chubby adult man in an aloha shirt and straw hat, always carrying his ukulele.
- Yotan: slim adult man with blond hair, black leather rock outfit, sunglasses, and guitar.
- Fukuchan: stylish adult man with neck badge and SPONSOR strap; he uses his Gyun-Gyun pose with both hands against his cheeks.
- Yametaro: stylized adult man with purple shirt, round glasses, and unchanged character design.
- The izakaya server is an anonymous fictional adult employee, shown mostly in side profile or from behind. The server is a one-scene comic catalyst, not a recurring villain.
- All drinking characters are adults. No intoxication, coercion, injury, realistic fire, gore, bullying, or depressing betrayal.

## Continuity and prop ledger

- Giant mug liquid level: EMPTY at Clip 1 start and end; fills from EMPTY to FULL during Clip 2; becomes ONE SIP LOWER during Clip 3; remains ONE SIP LOWER through Clip 4; appears empty and clean as Sobaya's ceremonial scepter in Clip 5 after the location-change cut.
- Beverage can: unopened and hidden below the counter at Clip 1 start; held upright below the counter at Clip 1 end; opened and poured into the EMPTY mug during Clip 2; visibly emptier and hidden again at Clip 2 end; never used for direct drinking.
- Sobaya never drinks from the can. The server never pours into an already-full mug.
- Mask color: white in Clips 1-2; white at Clip 3 start; vivid red at Clip 3 end and throughout Clips 4-5.
