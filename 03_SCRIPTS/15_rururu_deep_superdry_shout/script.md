# Video Script: "Rururu Deep Voice Shock"

## Format

- Length: 15 seconds
- Structure: 3 clips x 5 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Style: comedic office slice-of-life commercial, anime-illustration look
- Product: renewed craft beer "金曜日のルルル"
- Output design: each clip uses a fixed first frame and last frame for CapCut / Seedance 2.0 first-last-frame generation.

## Story summary

Tokun and Nako-sama are drinking "金曜日のルルル" just like the previous commercial. Sobaya appears, discovers the bottle, and cartoonishly drains one whole bottle. Tokun and Nako-sama catch him in the act. Sobaya then drains the beer in his giant mug, strikes a dramatic old-school commercial pose, and shouts in a deep voice: "アスゥアヒィスゥウプゥウアアドォルゥアイ！"

## Character and product invariants

- Nako-sama: white rabbit ears, wavy white hair, blue floral kimono, energetic craft beer creator. She may hold a slim glass of rosy beer.
- Tokun: aloha shirt, straw hat, ukulele, cheerful Hawaiian-loving president.
- Sobaya: tall muscular adult man, white mask, short-sleeve T-shirt, giant beer mug. The mask and mug must remain visible.
- Product: "金曜日のルルル" is a rosé-colored craft beer in a brown bottle with a red label and white rabbit silhouette.
- Tone: adults only, cartoon exaggeration, no intoxication, no danger, no pressure to drink. The joke is a wrong-brand shout caused by Sobaya's beer-commercial brain, not a criticism of "金曜日のルルル."
