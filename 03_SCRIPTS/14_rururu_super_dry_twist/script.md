# Video Script: "Friday Rururu, One-Bottle Shock"

## Format

- Length: 15 seconds
- Structure: 3 clips x 5 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Genre: comedic office slice-of-life commercial
- Product: renewed craft beer "金曜日のルルル"
- Core story formula: someone starts a strange tasting session, friends get pulled in, Sobaya causes a small fuss, everyone ends on a bright comedic smile.

## Character and product invariants

- Nako-sama: white rabbit ears, wavy white hair, blue floral kimono, energetic craft beer creator. She may hold a rosé beer glass or the product bottle.
- Tokun: aloha shirt, straw hat, ukulele, cheerful Hawaiian-loving president.
- Sobaya: muscular 41-year-old man, white mask, short-sleeve T-shirt, giant beer mug. Keep his mask and mug visible whenever possible.
- Product: "金曜日のルルル" is a rosé-colored craft beer in a brown bottle with a red label and white rabbit silhouette. The actual label text should be handled carefully in editing if exact lettering is needed.
- Safety tone: all characters are adults, the drinking is cartoonishly exaggerated, no intoxication, no danger, no pressure to drink.

With impossible cartoon speed, he grabs one bottle, tilts it back, and drains it in one exaggerated gulp. Tokun and Nako-sama notice him mid-gulp, stunned but amused rather than angry.

## Clip 3: The wrong-brand punchline

### Time

0:10-0:15

### Screen action

Sobaya finishes the bottle, then immediately raises his giant beer mug. Tokun and Nako-sama lean in, expecting a comment about "金曜日のルルル." Instead, Sobaya drains the mug in one huge comedic gulp, plants the mug on the table with a proud heroic pose, and shouts the wrong beer name in a deep dramatic voice. Tokun drops a ukulele chord in shock. Nako-sama laughs. End on the "金曜日のルルル" bottle and rosy beer glass as the product hero shot, with everyone smiling in the background.

### Dialogue

- Tokun: "で、味は？"
- Nako-sama: "金曜日のルルル、どうですか？"
- Sobaya: "アサヒスーパードライ！"

## Production notes

- If exact product label text is important, use the provided product reference photo or composited label text during final editing, because image generation may distort small lettering.
- Sobaya's final line should be delivered with an unexpectedly deep, dramatic, old-school commercial voice.
- The joke should feel like Sobaya loves beer so much that his brain briefly turns into a classic beer commercial, not like criticism of "金曜日のルルル."
- Keep the final emotional beat cheerful: Tokun is surprised, Nako-sama laughs, and Sobaya is proud.
