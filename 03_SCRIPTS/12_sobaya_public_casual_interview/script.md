## Clip 1 — The Entire Public Interview (0:00–0:15)

### Visual Action and Dialogue Timing

- **0.00–2.10:** Wide stage shot. Fukuchan steps forward, smiles at the audience, and puts both hands beside his cheeks in the Gyun-Gyun pose. He says quickly and clearly: “お時間なので、そば屋の公開カジュアル面談、始まりまギュン！”
- **2.10–4.15:** Snap cut to the interviewer and Sobaya on stools. The interviewer gestures politely toward the enormous mug and says: “面談中もビールは手放さないんですね。” Sobaya replies: “ええ、必須です。” He immediately takes a large gulp; the mug becomes empty.
- **4.15–7.55:** Jump cut; Sobaya’s mug is magically full again. The interviewer asks: “普段は何を？” Sobaya answers proudly: “ビールを飲みながら、動画とゲームを作ってます。” He immediately drains the mug again.
- **7.55–10.35:** Jump cut; the mug is full again. The interviewer blurts: “え、お仕事は？” Sobaya answers with total confidence: “窓際は、働かないことが仕事です。” He immediately drinks again. The interviewer freezes with a strained polite smile while a silent inner reaction is conveyed only through his eyes: he must not hire this man.
- **10.35–12.90:** Fukuchan pops between them in the Gyun-Gyun pose and says: “もう時間ギュン！選考フローをお願いギュン。” The interviewer answers apologetically: “あ、今ちょうど枠が埋まりました。”
- **12.90–15.00:** Fukuchan responds with a cheerful pun: “それはギュン念。” Sobaya raises a magically refilled mug and says: “とりあえず飲み行こう！” Fukuchan faces the audience, stretches the final word playfully, and says: “ご参加、ありがギューン！” The navy theater curtain rapidly closes from both sides and meets in the center exactly at the end, followed by one upbeat closing chime.
